Lienzo UI Community - Guidelines (summary, version 1.0)
Full text: https://github.com/deritu-kutsut/lienzo-ui/blob/main/docs/COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md

DO
- Share your own work, or work you may share, and say so truthfully.
- Declare a licence and, when there is one, a source link for every item. Give the credit the licence asks for.
- Describe items honestly. Mark adult content as 18+.
- Say when AI made a significant part of it. You are still responsible for the rights.
- Report problems with the Report button.

DON'T
- Upload fonts, images, icons or code you cannot share (ripped from games, single-user licences, paid packs).
- Hide a licence or a source, or remove an author's credit.
- Upload anything unsafe: hidden code, obfuscated scripts, files that are not what they claim.
- Post hate or harassment, or any sexual content involving minors (real, drawn or AI-generated).
- Impersonate people, brands, the project or the moderators.
- Share other people's personal data, or your own keys and tokens.
- Promote real-money gambling, scams or pay-to-earn schemes.
- Spam, use several accounts, dodge limits, or file false reports.
