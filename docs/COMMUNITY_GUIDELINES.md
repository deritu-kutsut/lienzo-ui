# Lienzo UI Community Guidelines

**Version 1.0** · Last updated: 2026-11-19 · Part of the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).

> **Draft, not legal advice.** The maintainer should have a lawyer review these guidelines for their jurisdiction.

The Community is a shared shelf of things people use in their own game projects. These rules keep it safe and useful. When in doubt, **do not upload it**, or ask first in a GitHub issue.

## Do

- **Share your own work**, or work you are allowed to share, and say so truthfully.
- **Declare a licence** for everything you upload: CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, MIT, Apache-2.0, OFL (fonts), or "proprietary, free to use" (your own terms, free in any project: write them in the description). Pick one that lets other users use the file in their projects. "All rights reserved" is not an option here.
- **Add a source link** when the work builds on something that has a page (the original font, the asset pack, your repository, the tutorial it came from).
- **Give credit** the licence asks for, in the description.
- **Describe it honestly**: what it is, what it needs, what it does. Use clear titles and relevant tags (up to 8).
- **Mark adult content as 18+.** If a reasonable person would call it adult, flag it.
- **Report** what breaks the rules (Report button on every item). Reports are how this stays clean.
- **Be kind** to other creators and users.

## Don't

| Don't | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Upload copyrighted work you cannot share** | Fonts ripped from a game or bought under a single-user licence; icons or sprites copied from a commercial game; screenshots of other people's UI re-packaged as templates; "free" versions of paid asset-store packs. |
| **Hide the licence or the source** | Re-uploading a CC BY font without credit; saying "original" about something you downloaded; removing the author's name from a file. |
| **Upload anything unsafe** | Plugins that fetch hidden code, obfuscated scripts, files that are not what the extension says, anything that tries to leave the app's sandbox. |
| **Post hate or harassment** | Slurs, attacks on a group, targeting a person, content made to bully. |
| **Post sexual content involving minors** | Any depiction, drawn, generated or real. This leads to an immediate ban and a report to the authorities. |
| **Post unmarked 18+ content** | Nudity, graphic violence or sexual themes without the 18+ flag. |
| **Impersonate** | Using a famous creator's or studio's name, logo or style to pass as them; names like "Official", "Admin" or "Support"; pretending to be the moderation team. |
| **Share other people's personal data** | Names, addresses, phone numbers, private chats, real faces without consent, anything that doxxes. Do not put your own secrets (keys, tokens) in files either. |
| **Promote gambling or money schemes** | Real-money gambling, casino or betting templates that point to real operators, pay-to-earn or pyramid schemes, giveaways that ask for money or data, crypto "investment" content. Game UIs that simulate in-game currency are fine. |
| **Spam or abuse the system** | Dozens of near-identical uploads, keyword-stuffed titles, fake hearts or follows, using several accounts, trying to dodge limits or a ban. |
| **File false reports** | Reporting something you know is fine, or claiming copyright on work that is not yours. Repeated false claims lose the right to file them. |

## Fonts, images and templates must declare their licence and source

Every item has a **licence** and, when there is one, a **source link**. Moderators check them. A few rules of thumb:

- **Fonts:** most free fonts use the OFL or a similar licence that allows embedding in games. Check the font's own licence file. If the licence forbids redistribution, you may not upload the font here.
- **Images and icons:** your own art, public-domain or CC0 art, or art under a licence that lets others reuse it (with credit when required). Screenshots and "inspired by" art are not a way around somebody else's copyright.
- **Templates and themes:** the layout and your own art are yours to share. If a template includes someone else's font or image, their licence still applies and you must declare it.
- **Plugins:** declare the code licence (MIT, Apache-2.0...). Do not include code you cannot relicense.

## AI-generated content

AI-generated content is allowed **if you have the right to share it** and you tell the truth about it.

- Say it is AI-generated in the description when AI made a significant part of it.
- You are responsible for it like any other upload: it must not copy a recognisable protected work, a real person's likeness without consent, or a brand's logo or characters.
- Check the terms of the tool you used. Some tools do not allow commercial use or redistribution.
- Do not claim AI-generated work as hand-made, and do not use AI to mass-produce near-duplicates for spam.
- Sexual content involving minors is banned even if it is fully AI-generated.

## 18+ content

- Content that is adult (nudity, strong sexual themes, graphic violence) must be flagged **18+**. Automatic moderation or a moderator can add the flag, but nobody can take it off.
- 18+ content is shown only to accounts that declared they are 18 or older and turned the filter on. It still has to follow every other rule here: no sexual content involving minors, no illegal content.

## Reporting and what happens

- Use **Report** on an item and choose a reason: copyright, adult content, malware, hate or harassment, spam, impersonation, personal data, or other. Add a short note (up to 500 characters).
- For copyright, use **Report > Copyright**: the item is hidden at once until a moderator decides. See the [Copyright and Takedown Policy](COPYRIGHT.md). You can also use the GitHub issue templates (content report, copyright notice).
- Moderators remove, hide or restrict what breaks these rules, and may give strikes or ban accounts. Details and appeals: [ENFORCEMENT.md](ENFORCEMENT.md).

## Quick summary

Share what is yours (or free to share), say where it came from and under which licence, keep it safe and honest, flag adult content, respect people, and report problems.
