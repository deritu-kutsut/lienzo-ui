# Lienzo UI Community: Copyright and Takedown Policy

**Version 1.0** · Last updated: 2026-11-19 · Part of the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).

> **Draft, not legal advice.** The maintainer should have a lawyer review this policy for their jurisdiction. It follows the general shape of notice-and-takedown systems (such as the US DMCA and the EU Digital Services Act) but it does **not** by itself make the service eligible for any legal safe harbour: that may require registering a designated agent or meeting other formal requirements where the maintainer lives.

We respect creators. Uploading content you do not have the right to share is not allowed. If something in the Community infringes your copyright, tell us and we will act quickly.

## 1. How to report infringing content

Either way works:

1. **In the app:** open the item, press **Report > Copyright**, and describe the work and why it is infringed (10 to 2000 characters), plus your name or the rights holder's name (up to 200 characters). The item is **hidden immediately**, before anyone decides. To limit abuse, in-app claims need an account at least **24 hours old** that has accepted the Terms; if a single creator receives more than 3 open claims within 24 hours, the extra claims are recorded and flagged for a moderator but do **not** hide the item automatically.
2. **On GitHub:** open an issue with the **Copyright notice** template: <https://github.com/deritu-kutsut/lienzo-ui/issues/new?template=copyright-notice.md>. Do not put private details (home address, phone) in a public issue: say that you will provide them privately and we will arrange a way.

## 2. What a notice must contain

A notice is complete when it includes:

1. Who you are: your name, and the rights holder's name if you act for someone else, and a way to contact you.
2. **Identification of the copyrighted work** that you say is infringed (a link to the original, a registration, a description).
3. **Identification of the item** in the Community (its title, its creator's public name, and the item link or id).
4. A statement that you have a **good-faith belief** that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
5. A statement, **under penalty of perjury where that applies**, that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.
6. Your **signature** (a typed full name is enough).

We may ask for missing information. Incomplete notices may be rejected.

> **Warning about false notices.** Knowingly claiming copyright over something you do not own, or claiming infringement in bad faith, can make you liable for damages and costs in some jurisdictions, and **we will ignore further claims from accounts that file repeated false notices** (in the app, three rejected claims in 30 days block further in-app claims).

## 3. What we do with a notice

1. **Hide first.** The item disappears from the catalog straight away and its public files are moved out of public view. This does not mean we decided it is infringing.
2. **Let the uploader know.** The uploader sees in the app that the item was hidden because of a copyright claim, and the substance of the notice (what work is claimed and why) is available to them in their data export and on request through a GitHub issue, so they can answer it. The service has no email, so nobody is notified by mail. The claimant's contact details are not shared publicly.
3. **A moderator reviews it.** The moderator compares the claim, the item, the declared licence and source, and the uploader's answer.
4. **Outcome.**
   - **Upheld:** the item is removed permanently and the uploader gets a **strike**.
   - **Rejected:** the item is restored.
   - **Counter-notice:** see section 4.

## 4. Counter-notice (for uploaders)

If your content was hidden or removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you have the right to share it, you can send a counter-notice by opening a GitHub issue with the **Content report** template (choose "Counter-notice") or the text below. It must contain:

1. Your name and a way to reach you.
2. Identification of the item that was hidden or removed and where it appeared.
3. A statement, **under penalty of perjury where that applies**, that you have a good-faith belief the content was hidden by mistake or misidentification, or that you have the right to share it.
4. Your agreement to be bound by the jurisdiction of your courts or, if outside the claimant's country, to accept that the claimant can pursue the claim there, to the extent your law requires such a statement.
5. Your signature (a typed full name).

**What happens next:**

- We forward the substance of your counter-notice to the claimant (through the issue or the channel they gave) and record it.
- Unless the claimant shows within **14 calendar days** of your counter-notice (the system's waiting period, about 10 business days) that they have started a court action against you, we **restore the item**. If you edited the title, description or tags while it was hidden, the edited version is not republished automatically: it goes through the normal review first.
- If the claimant does start legal action, the item stays down until the matter is resolved or withdrawn.

### Counter-notice template

```text
Counter-notice

Item (title, my public name, link or id):
What was hidden / removed and when:

I, [full name], state that I have a good-faith belief that this content was hidden or
removed because of a mistake or misidentification, or that I have the right to share it, because:
[explain: I created it / licence and source / permission obtained, with links]

I understand the claimant may respond. [Statement about jurisdiction, if your law requires one.]

Contact (do not post private details publicly; say how we can reach you):
Signature: [typed full name]      Date:
```

## 5. Repeat-infringer policy

- Each **upheld** claim gives the uploader one **strike**.
- At **3 strikes**, the account's **uploads are disabled permanently** (a ban that is lifted only through an appeal; see [ENFORCEMENT.md](ENFORCEMENT.md)). Existing approved content that was not claimed may stay, or be reviewed, at a moderator's discretion.
- Deleting the account or opening another one to continue after a ban is a violation of the Terms.
- Serious or deliberate infringement can lead to an immediate ban without waiting for three strikes.

## 6. Other rights

Trademark, privacy and personality-right complaints, and anything that is not copyright, use the **Content report** template or **Report** in the app. We handle them under the [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md). If you are in a country with other notice rules, tell us in the issue and we will follow what the law requires of us.

## 7. Our records

We keep claim records (including the claimant details you give) for up to 3 years after resolution, to apply the repeat-infringer policy and to defend against disputes. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
